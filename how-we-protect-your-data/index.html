A €400 day rate in Ireland equals about €92,000 per year, based on 5 days a week over 46 working weeks (before tax).

How a day rate converts to a salary

The maths is simple — the value is in choosing realistic inputs. Your gross annual income is your day rate multiplied by the days you work each week and the number of weeks you actually work in a year:

Annual = Day rate × Days per week × Working weeks

Permanent employees are usually paid across 52 weeks including paid holidays. Contractors and freelancers are not paid for time off, so using the full 52 weeks overstates your real income. A range of 44–48 weeks is more honest once you allow for annual leave, Irish bank holidays and any gaps between contracts. This calculator defaults to 46.

Remember the figure above is gross — before income tax, USC and PRSI. To see what actually lands in your account, use the net salary calculator linked below.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a day rate to an annual salary in Ireland?

Multiply your day rate by the days you work per week, then by your working weeks per year. A €400 day rate at 5 days over 46 weeks is €92,000 gross.

How many weeks should a contractor use?

Use 44–48 weeks instead of 52 to account for unpaid holidays, bank holidays and gaps between contracts. 46 is a common realistic default.

Is this before or after tax?

Before tax (gross). For take-home pay after PAYE, USC and PRSI, use the Ireland net salary calculator.
