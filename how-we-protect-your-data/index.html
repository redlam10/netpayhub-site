How We Protect Your Data

Quick answer Your salary never leaves your browser. Every NetPayHub calculator runs entirely on your device, the page is technically blocked from sending any data out (connect-src 'none'), and nothing is stored — no accounts, no tracking, no third-party scripts.

Salary and tax figures are some of the most sensitive numbers you own. Most online calculators send what you type to a server to be processed. We built NetPayHub so that is impossible.

What is a Content-Security-Policy, and why it matters here

A Content-Security-Policy (CSP) is a rule set the browser enforces on a page. Ours tells your browser to refuse any network connection the page tries to make. Even if a bug or a malicious script attempted to upload your salary, the browser would block it. This is the difference between promising privacy and enforcing it.

How this works with Irish tax data

Our calculators use the official 2026 bands from Revenue.ie and Budget 2026 — the standard rate cut-off, USC bands, PRSI rate and tax credits. Those rules are public and built into the page. Your personal numbers are matched against them locally. So you get an accurate take-home pay figure for PAYE, USC and PRSI without ever revealing your income to us or anyone else.

Frequently asked questions

Does NetPayHub store my salary?

No. Nothing you type is stored. No accounts, no database of inputs. Close or refresh the page and your figures disappear.

Can my salary be sent to a server?

No. The connect-src 'none' policy blocks the page from opening any connection, and all calculation is local.

Do you use tracking or ads that read my inputs?

No third-party scripts run on the calculators. The site is served over HTTPS with strict security headers (HSTS, no-referrer, frame-deny).
