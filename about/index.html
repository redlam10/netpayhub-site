About NetPayHub

NetPayHub builds privacy-first salary and tax calculators. Every calculation runs in your browser using official Revenue figures, so you get accurate estimates without your income data ever leaving your device.

Why we built it

Most online salary tools quietly send what you type to their servers, ad networks or analytics partners. We think working out your own pay shouldn't cost you your privacy. NetPayHub does the maths locally on your device and is technically blocked from sending your numbers anywhere.
