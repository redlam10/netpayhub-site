Ireland 2026 tax rates at a glance

Source: Budget 2026 & Revenue.ie. Figures shown for a single PAYE worker; married/civil-partner thresholds and credits differ.
Charge2026 rate & bands
Income tax (PAYE)20% up to €44,000 (single) / €53,000 (married, one income); 40% on the balance. Tax credits: Personal €2,000 + Employee (PAYE) €2,000.
USCExempt under €13,000. Otherwise 0.5% to €12,012 · 2% to €28,700 · 3% to €70,044 · 8% above.
PRSI (Class A)4.2% of gross pay, rising to 4.35% from 1 October 2026.

Worked example: on a €50,000 single salary you'd pay about €7,200 income tax, €1,033 USC and €2,100 PRSI — leaving roughly €39,667 take-home (€3,306/month). Run your own figure →
