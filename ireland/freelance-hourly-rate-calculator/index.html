How the freelance hourly rate calculator works

Charging your "salary divided by hours" is the classic freelancer mistake — it ignores overhead and the days you can't bill. This tool works backwards from what you want to keep:

Revenue = Target income × (1 + Overhead%) · Day rate = Revenue ÷ Billable days · Hourly = Day rate ÷ 8

To keep €60,000, add 20% overhead to reach €72,000 of revenue, then spread it over 220 billable days — about €327 a day, or €41 an hour on an 8-hour day. Pure arithmetic, no tax data, so it never goes stale.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I charge as a freelancer in Ireland?

Add overhead to the income you want, then divide by billable days. €60,000 + 20% = €72,000 ÷ 220 days ≈ €327/day (~€41/hour).

How many billable days should I use?

After weekends, holidays, admin and sales, many full-time freelancers bill 200–230 days a year. 220 is a realistic default.

What should overhead include?

Software, equipment, insurance, accounting, pension, training and unpaid time — anything you must cover before you keep a cent.
