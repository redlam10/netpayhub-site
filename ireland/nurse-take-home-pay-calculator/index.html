How a nurse's take-home pay is worked out (2026)

Nurses are PAYE employees, so the same three deductions apply to gross pay: income tax, USC and PRSI, with the €2,000 Personal and €2,000 Employee tax credits. This calculator uses the Budget 2026 bands.

Charge2026 basis
Income tax20% to €44,000 (single), 40% above; €4,000 credits
USC0.5% / 2% / 3% / 8% bands; exempt ≤ €13,000
PRSI (Class A)4.2% of gross

Staff nurse pay scale (2026)

Under the June 2026 consolidated HSE pay scales, a staff nurse starts at about €37,800 (Point 1) and moves up one increment a year across roughly 12 points into the mid-€50,000s — before premiums. Night, weekend and public-holiday shift premiums, plus location and qualification allowances, typically add a meaningful amount on top of basic pay, so enter your full expected gross above. People also ask

What is the take-home pay for a nurse on €45,000?

About €37,027 a year (~€3,086/month) for a single nurse after income tax (€5,200), USC (€883) and PRSI (€1,890) in 2026, before premiums or pension.

Is shift or overtime pay taxed differently?

No — it's taxed like normal pay, but as it sits on top of your salary it's usually taxed at your marginal rate. Add it to your salary figure to see the effect.

Can a pension cut a nurse's tax?

Yes — pension contributions get income-tax relief at your marginal rate. Enter a pension % above, or use the pension tax relief calculator.
