How the salary to hourly calculator works in Ireland

To turn a yearly salary into an hourly rate, divide it by the hours you actually work in a year:

Hourly = Annual ÷ (Hours per week × Weeks per year)

For a €41,600 salary at 40 hours a week across 52 weeks, that's €20.00 an hour. The daily rate divides your weekly pay (€800) across a standard 5-day week, giving €160 a day. It's pure arithmetic with no tax data, so it never expires.

These are gross figures. For take-home pay after income tax, USC and PRSI, use the Ireland net salary calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert an annual salary to an hourly rate?

Divide the salary by your yearly hours (hours per week × weeks per year). €41,600 ÷ (40 × 52) = €20.00 per hour.

How is the daily rate calculated?

We split your weekly pay across a 5-day week. €41,600 over 52 weeks is €800 a week, or €160 a day.

Is this gross or net?

Gross (before tax). For take-home pay, use the net salary calculator.
