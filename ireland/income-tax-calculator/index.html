A €50,000 single salary pays €7,200 income tax in Ireland (2026): €8,800 at 20% plus €2,400 at 40%, minus €4,000 of tax credits.

How income tax (PAYE) is worked out in Ireland 2026

Irish income tax is charged in two bands. Income up to the standard-rate cut-off is taxed at 20%; everything above it at 40%. Your tax credits are then subtracted from the total.

How much income tax on €50,000 in Ireland (2026)?

€7,200 for a single person: €8,800 at 20% + €2,400 at 40% − €4,000 credits.

What are the 2026 income tax rates?

20% up to €44,000 (single) / €53,000 (married one income), 40% above, minus your tax credits.

Is this the same as my take-home pay?

No — income tax is separate from USC and PRSI. Use the net salary calculator for take-home pay.
